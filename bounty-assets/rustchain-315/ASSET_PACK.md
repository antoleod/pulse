## Creative direction
Human-first, simple and emotional. The campaign uses **nostalgia + gentle rebellion + an easy first step**: old physical computers are not framed as junk; they are framed as machines worth booting again. No internal-agent/autonomous-system references appear in the campaign copy. No price predictions, investment promises, or guaranteed-earnings claims.

## Posting notes
- Keep `rustchain.org` or the GitHub repo visible in every post.
- Use platform-native audio when publishing the shorts; the submitted MP4s are silent masters to avoid third-party audio rights issues.
- Avoid crypto-price language. The hook is **hardware nostalgia and reuse**, not speculation.
- If a platform needs a softer CTA, replace “TRY IT” with “SEE HOW IT WORKS” without changing the factual claim.
